Guide

How AI training data licensing works.

AI training data licensing is a written agreement in which the owner of text, images, audio, video, or data grants permission to use specific works to train a model, on defined terms.

Updated October 2026. Published by Credtent, a Delaware Public Benefit Corporation.

What rights does a training license grant?

A training license grants permission to use named works to train or fine-tune a model, within the scope the contract states. It does not automatically cover use in AI answers, and it does not transfer copyright.

A license also conveys only the rights its signatories hold. That is why a careful license comes with a manifest.

Our corporate page states the boundary in these words: "Licenses include the rights the contracting holders warranted, a named manifest, and a disclosure pack your counsel can use for EU AI Act Art. 53 and CA AB 2013 filings. Those statutes still bind the developer. Indemnity, if any, is only as written in the signed license."

Who can offer a license?

Individual creatives, publishers and other catalog owners, marketplaces, and collective programs. They differ in scale and in how payment is arranged. Credtent does not publish a rate card on this preview.

What records do developers need?

Developers of general-purpose AI models in the EU must publish a sufficiently detailed summary of training content and keep a copyright policy that honors rights reservations, under Article 53 of the EU AI Act. California's AB 2013 requires developers to post training-data documentation. A developer's filings remain the developer's responsibility.

How can a rights holder participate?

Document ownership, list the works, set which uses you will accept, and approve each agreement. You can also choose the free opt-out instead. Opt-Out Premium is US$49 a year for individuals and US$249+ a year for organizations. It adds auditing and does not include licensing.

Questions

Does a training license include answers and excerpts?

Not automatically. Inference, grounding, and display need their own written terms.

Can a license fix past training for everyone?

No. A covenant not to sue reaches only the rights holders who sign, and only the named works. It is not a regulator cure.
