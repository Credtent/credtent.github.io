Who Credtent is

E.R. Burgess and Dr. Galen Buckwalter.

Credtent connects AI companies and the creative industries through licensing, so AI solutions are ethical and commercially safe.

E.R. Burgess founded Credtent after more than eight years of research into creative rights and data provenance, well before the public AI debate grew loud. The starting idea was simple: AI and human creativity can work together, ethically, legally, and profitably, when the people who make the work are paid and their consent is on record.

E.R. Burgess brings over 30 years of industry experience. He is also a published tabletop game designer, with card, board, and role-playing games to his name. He leads Credtent as Founder and CEO, alongside Co-Founder and Chief Science Officer Dr. Galen Buckwalter.

Our mission is to bring AI and creativity together ethically, legally, and profitably. Credtent, Inc. is a Delaware Public Benefit Corporation and a Certified B Corp, so protecting creative rights in the AI economy is part of how the company is built.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the free opt-out do?

Credtent's free opt-out records your AI-training preferences and sends them to AI companies each quarter; it cannot guarantee every company acts on them.

Does registration remove work from datasets?

No. Registration does not automatically remove work from datasets. Credtent records preferences and the notices it sends.

What is Opt-Out Premium?

Optional monthly auditing of observable model responses. It is US$49 a year for individuals and US$299+ a year for organizations. The observations are not proof of dataset inclusion.
