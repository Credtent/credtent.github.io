Frequently asked questions

What does the free opt-out do?

Credtent's free opt-out records your AI-training preferences and sends them to AI companies each quarter. It cannot guarantee every company acts on them.

Does registration remove work from datasets?

No. Registration does not automatically remove work from datasets. Credtent records preferences and the notices it sends.

What is Opt-Out Premium?

Opt-Out Premium for rights holders enrolls their works in Credtent's periodic AI model audits, from US$299 a year. Individual creatives get the same audits with licensing, at US$49 a year for sign-ups through December 31, 2026. Audit results are not proof of dataset inclusion.
