Opt-out

Opt out of AI training. Free.

Credtent's free opt-out records your AI-training preferences and sends them to AI companies each quarter; it cannot guarantee every company acts on them.

Register your work once. Credtent records your reservation and delivers the notice to AI companies. No fee to decline AI use of your work.

Start your free opt-out

01

Create a free account

The opt-out account is free and stays free. There is no charge to decline AI use of your work.

02

Register your works

Add text, audio, video, image, or data and mark what you want in the opt-out request.

03

Credtent sends the notice

Credtent keeps a dated record of when each notice went out.

What you can count on

It is free, and the free path is not a trial. Credtent, Inc. is a Delaware Public Benefit Corporation. The record is evidence of notice, not proof that a company complied. Opt-Out Premium adds access to auditing and does not include licensing.

Questions about limits live in the opt-out limitations FAQ. Companies can read how AI companies work with Credtent.

Questions

Will every company act on the notice?

No. We cannot guarantee that every company will act on your request. Sent notices are distinct from confirmed receipt or action.

Does this remove work from existing datasets?

Registration does not automatically remove work from datasets.

Can I change my mind?

You can update preferences later. Opting out and licensing are separate choices.
