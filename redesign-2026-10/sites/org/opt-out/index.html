What the opt-out is

One registration, one notice to the industry.

When you register works with Credtent and choose opt-out, Credtent records your reservation of rights and delivers the notice to AI companies, with a dated record of when it went out. Instead of relying on each platform's separate setting, you set your preference once.

What you can count on

It is free, and it stays free.

Registering a work for the opt-out costs nothing and will stay free. It is a standing commitment tied to Credtent's structure as a Delaware Public Benefit Corporation, which legally obligates the company to protect creative rights. The free path is not a trial.

You can change your mind.

You can update your preferences at any time to enable licensing, with no retroactive fees and no penalties. A work that is opted out today can be made licensable tomorrow, and a licensable work can go back to opt-out.

It is recorded, not just declared.

Credtent keeps a dated record of when each notice went out. That record is evidence you can point to if your work is used anyway. It is evidence of notice, not proof that a company complied. Opt-Out Premium adds access to auditing and does not include licensing.

It can become an income stream.

The same registry that records your opt-out can license your work on your terms if you ever choose to opt in. Protect your voice, your image, and your style now, and keep the option to earn from AI licensing later.

Questions about limits live in the opt-out limitations FAQ.

For AI companies

Honoring opt-outs is a compliance asset.

Credtent keeps opt-out records that show good-faith compliance, alongside chain-of-title records and disclosure records that support EU AI Act and California AB 2013 filings. Ignoring a registered opt-out becomes a documented record; honoring one becomes part of a position you can defend.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will every company act on the notice?

No. We cannot guarantee that every company will act on your request. Sent notices are distinct from confirmed receipt or action.

Does this remove work from existing datasets?

Registration does not automatically remove work from datasets.

Can I change my mind?

You can update preferences later. Opting out and licensing are separate choices.
