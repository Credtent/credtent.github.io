What you can count on

It is free.

Registering a work for the opt-out costs nothing, and the opt-out is not a trial.

Credtent does not plan to charge for the free opt-out.

You can change your mind.

You can move a work between opt-out and licensing at any time, with no retroactive fees and no penalties.

It is recorded and dated.

Credtent keeps a dated record of each notice it sends. You can point to that record as evidence that the notice was sent.

The record does not confirm that a company received the notice or acted on it.

You can license later.

The same account that records your opt-out can license your work on your terms, if you choose to opt in.

Every paid plan keeps the same opt-out and adds enrollment in periodic AI model audits.

What the opt-out cannot do

For AI companies: honoring opt-outs.

Credtent keeps a dated record of each opt-out notice it sends, and companies that honor those notices can show that they did.

Frequently asked questions

Will every company act on the notice?

No. We cannot guarantee that every company will act on your request. Sent notices are distinct from confirmed receipt or action.

Does this remove work from existing datasets?

Registration does not automatically remove work from datasets.

Can I change my mind?

You can update preferences later. Opting out and licensing are separate choices.
