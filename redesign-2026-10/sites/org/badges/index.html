The badge systems

Three badge systems, one for each side of the rights economy. Certification looks at works and organizational processes.

For creatives and publishers

Creative Origin

Signals the role of AI in a specific creative work, for creatives and publishers who want the audience to know how a work was made, from a fully human-composed novel to an AI-generated image, with the middle ground disclosed honestly.

  • Human Composed: the work was composed by a person, without generative AI composing it.
  • AI-Assisted: a person made the work with help from AI tools.
  • AI-Generated: the work was generated by AI.
  • Legacy Creation: the work was made before November 2022, before generative AI tools.

Only Human Composed and AI-Assisted are offered as certified, with a corner ribbon. AI-Generated is not a certified tier.

There are two ways to display it: a free self-declared disclosure badge you make yourself, or a paid, expert-reviewed certified badge with a registry record. A free badge is a disclosure, not a certification.

For platforms

Creative Consent

Shows that a platform licenses and uses the work of creatives on rights-respecting terms: how it treats their data, content, and metadata, and whether it respects their choices about AI training.

  • Full Protection: the platform does not use members' content for AI training, does not use it internally for generative AI, and does not share it with third parties for AI training.
  • Optional Protection: the platform gives users a choice about AI training, with a clear, free, and simple opt-out.
  • No Protection: the platform uses members' content for AI training without consent or compensation and offers no opt-out.
