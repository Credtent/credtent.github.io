How does certification work?

Three steps to a verified human-authorship badge, built to stand up to studio, legal, and audience scrutiny and to fit a screenwriter's schedule.

01

Submit and survey

Upload your screenplay and complete a short authorship survey covering your process, drafts, and tools.

02

Secure analysis

AI-detection analysis runs in a closed environment. Your work is never used for training.

03

Expert validation

A professional auditor reviews the analysis and authorship record, then issues your Certified Human Composed badge.

Make human-authored the preferred pile.
