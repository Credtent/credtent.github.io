Writers Guild of America

Certified Human Composed for screenwriters.

The Writers Guild of America reviewed Credtent's screenplay certification process and accepted it. The badge is a record of review, not a promise about copyright registration.

Submit a screenplay and a short authorship survey. Analysis runs in a closed environment and the work is not used for training. A professional auditor reviews the record and can issue a Certified Human Composed badge.

The badge is meant for end-credit treatment and a documentation packet. It does not guarantee a copyright registration.

Member and regular prices are not published on this page.
