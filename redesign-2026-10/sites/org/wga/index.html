A certification service for screenwriters.

Credtent's certification process reviews your authorship record and issues a Certified Human Composed badge when the work meets the Human Composed standard. It gives you a documented record of how the work was made, and it does not change your legal rights. Production companies and studios need assurance that screenplays are human-authored to avoid copyright and IP risk.

How does certification work?

Three steps lead to a Certified Human Composed badge, and the process is built to fit a screenwriter's schedule.

01

Submit and survey

Upload your screenplay and complete a short authorship survey covering your process, drafts, and tools.

02

Secure analysis

An AI-detection analysis informs the review. A professional auditor makes the decision. Credtent does not use your screenplay to train AI models.

03

Expert validation

A professional auditor reviews the analysis and authorship record, then issues your Certified Human Composed badge.
