Frequently Asked Questions

How much does Credtent cost?

It depends on which side of the market you are on. AI companies pay for custom licensing priced per deal. Content partners keep 85% of every licensing deal Credtent closes, with Credtent keeping 15%, and a platform fee applies. Individual creatives can opt out for free, enroll their works in periodic AI model audits with Opt-Out Premium, or license their work with a licensing plan.

How much does Credtent cost for AI companies?

There is no fixed public price. Licenses are custom agreements scoped to the content, the license type, and the size of the organization, and each one includes a disclosure pack. Pricing is set in a scoping conversation, not from a rate card.

How much does Credtent cost for content partners?

A platform fee applies, and Credtent keeps 15% of each licensing deal it closes and sends the other 85% to the rights holder under the Starter Partner terms. The Organization License keeps the same split and adds more services for larger catalogs, and Enterprise is priced to the catalog.

What does Opt-Out Premium cost?

US$49 annually for individuals and US$299+ annually for organizations.

What does the book cost?

The published collaborithmic.com prices are $15 for the e-book, $25 for the paperback, and $50 for the living e-book.
