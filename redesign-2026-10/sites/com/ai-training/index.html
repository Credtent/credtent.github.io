Ongoing relationships can provide access to new human-created and expert work. The agreement defines which updates, renewals, and payments apply.

A training license does not automatically require recurring payments for every continued model use. Specific contracts define the scope, duration, compensation, and renewal arrangements.

Origins and selection deserve care.

Research on recursive training highlights why the composition and handling of training data matter. Findings depend on the experimental conditions.

Nature / 2024

Recursive replacement

Shumailov and colleagues observed degradation when models were recursively trained on generated data under the conditions they studied.

Read Shumailov et al.

Research / 2024

Retaining original data

Gerstgrasser and colleagues found that retaining original real data while accumulating synthetic data avoided collapse in their experiments.

Read Gerstgrasser et al.

These studies support attention to data origins, diversity, and careful selection. They do not show that all synthetic data is harmful, that human authorship alone guarantees quality, or that buying a license prevents model collapse.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the license include?

Licenses include the rights the contracting holders warranted, a named manifest, and a disclosure pack your counsel can use for EU AI Act Art. 53 and CA AB 2013 filings. Those statutes still bind the developer. Indemnity, if any, is only as written in the signed license.

Does a training license require payment forever?

A training license does not automatically require recurring payments for every continued model use. Specific contracts define the scope, duration, compensation, and renewal arrangements.

Do the research notes prove a license prevents model collapse?

No. The studies support attention to data origins and careful selection. They do not show that buying a license prevents model collapse.
