Creative Origin

Creative Origin

Expert review of how work is made.

Creative Origin certification reviews an organization's editorial process. It is not an automated detector score and it does not promise a legal outcome.

What the badge means

A verified process, not just a score.

The Creative Origin badge certifies that published content was conceived, written, and edited by human authors and editors. Credtent's independent reviewers verified the process behind it, not just the finished text. It is not an AI detector and not a checklist. It is a verified record of human craft.

The review is about process and disclosure, not a promise that every future work is human-made, and it does not promise a legal outcome.

  • Human-authored and human-edited content
  • Expert review at every editorial stage
  • Clear disclosure of any AI tool use
  • An audit-ready editorial chain of custody
  • A verified organizational process, not a single piece
  • Annual renewal as standards change

What does a Creative Origin review check?

Human authorship

Reviewers confirm the content was conceived and written by human authors. Author attestations are kept in the certification record.

Editorial process

We review the full editorial workflow: assignment, drafting, editing, fact-checking, and publication.

AI use disclosed

Any AI tools used in the process are identified and disclosed in the certification record.

An independent reviewer

Reviews are carried out by Credtent-trained practitioners from editorial, journalism, and media backgrounds. You get a named reviewer, not a queue.

A public record

Every badge links to a public certification record in the Credtent registry, so readers, buyers, and platforms can check it.

Annual renewal

Renewal keeps the certification current and the badge valid across distribution partners and platforms.

Who does Credtent certify?

Any organization that publishes human-authored work, across media, journalism, trade publications, newsletters, and digital content.

Trade and specialty media

Industry publications, trade magazines, and vertical outlets that publish expert editorial content.

News and journalism

Digital newsrooms, regional papers, and independent journalism committed to human reporting and editorial accountability.

Books and long-form publishing

Trade publishers, academic presses, and independent houses. Credtent certifies publisher editorial processes today; certification for individual books is in development.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Creative Origin an automated score?

No. AI detectors score finished text for statistical signatures and can be fooled. Credtent reviews the editorial process behind the content: who wrote it, who edited it, what tools were used, and how oversight was kept at every stage. A self-declared badge from the badge builder is a disclosure, not this review.

Does certification require that no AI was used?

No. If AI tools were used anywhere in the process, they are identified and disclosed in the certification record. Transparency is required; absence of AI is not.

How long does certification take to start?

Our reviewer team contacts you within five business days of receiving your process documentation, and comes back with a certification scope and fee estimate.
