Creative Origin

Expert review of how work is made.

Creative Origin certification reviews an organization's editorial process. It is not an automated detector score and it does not promise a legal outcome.

Publishers, studios, and other organizations that publish human-authored work can ask Credtent to review the editorial process behind that work. The review is about process and disclosure, not a promise that every future work is human-made.

Creative Origin sits alongside Creative Consent for platforms and the badge overview on credtent.org/badges.
