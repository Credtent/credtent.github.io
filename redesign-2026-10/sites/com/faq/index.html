Frequently Asked Questions

What is Credtent?

Credtent connects AI companies and the creative industries through licensing, so AI solutions are ethical and commercially safe. Creatives and rights holders register their work for opt-out, licensing, or both. Credtent builds no AI models and takes no equity in either side.

What does a Credtent license cover?

Licenses include the rights the contracting holders warranted, a named manifest, and a disclosure pack your counsel can use for EU AI Act Art. 53 and CA AB 2013 filings. Those statutes still bind the developer. Indemnity, if any, is only as written in the signed license.

What about content we already trained on?

We can structure a going-forward license plus a covenant not to sue from the rights holders who sign, covering the named works. That is not a release from everyone else, and it is not a regulator cure.

How long does a deal take?

A 20-minute scoping call leads to a proposal within 5 business days. Most deals close in 2 to 6 weeks, depending on the content and how many rights holders are involved. Single-catalog deals close faster; larger, multi-format collections take longer.

Why license instead of scraping public content?

Because the legal risk is real and growing. Active court cases are testing the use of scraped training data, and new disclosure rules leave a paper trail that makes undocumented scraping visible. Ignoring a registered opt-out also becomes a documented record.

How should we read a valuation?

A baseline valuation comes when you add content; optional paid custom valuation goes deeper. It is an informed starting point, not a guaranteed price.

Do I give up ownership of my content?

No. Credtent acts as a licensing agent, not a publisher or co-owner. You keep full ownership of everything you list. Credtent holds only limited representation rights for AI licensing, and you can end that at any time. Listing does not transfer copyright, exclusivity, or any ownership to Credtent.

How does the revenue split work?

Every licensing deal Credtent closes sends 85% of gross revenue to the rights holders and data providers whose content was licensed. Credtent keeps 15% for its platform, legal work, compliance tools, and operations. The split is on gross revenue, not net.

When do I get paid?

Credtent pays royalties quarterly. Each payment comes with a usage report showing which licensees used your catalog and how much, so you can see which deals earned your share.

Can I choose which AI companies license my content?

Yes. When you register your catalog, you set limits by vertical, use case, and type of application. You can exclude specific licensees or whole categories, and every deal needs your approval before it goes ahead.

What kinds of content can I list?

Text, audio, video, images, and structured data, across education, healthcare, sports, entertainment, and media. You can register a mixed-format catalog in one place.

Is Credtent tied to any AI company or big-tech platform?

No. Credtent does not build models, take equity positions, or share data outside of licensed transactions.

Where is Credtent incorporated?

Credtent, Inc. is a Delaware Public Benefit Corporation. Its charter names two public benefits: to empower and protect creators of original works in the marketplace of artificial intelligence, and to promote the ethical and credible use and deployment of AI.

Where do creative opt-out questions live?

Questions about the free opt-out, what it can and cannot do, and switching to licensing later are answered at credtent.org/opt-out and credtent.org/resources/opt-out-limitations.
