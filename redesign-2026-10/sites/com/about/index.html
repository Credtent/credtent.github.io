About Credtent

Credtent, Inc. is a Delaware Public Benefit Corporation founded by E.R. Burgess (CEO) and Dr. Galen Buckwalter.

More choice. Wider participation. We help creatives and AI companies find constructive ways to work together through choice, consent, credit, and compensation.

The origin

Why was Credtent founded?

Credtent exists because the AI economy is not a zero-sum game. Creatives and AI companies need each other, and they need a neutral place to do business together, ethically, legally, and profitably.

The problem Credtent solves was visible long before it became a headline. For more than eight years, our team researched creative IP, digital rights, and the coming collision between AI's demand for training data and intellectual property law. By the time AI content licensing reached the mainstream, Credtent already had a thesis, a model, and a growing set of partners. We weren't reacting to a crisis. We were preparing for one we could see coming.

What is Credtent's mission?

To bring AI and creativity together ethically, legally, and profitably. We hold every decision to that. If a deal does not work for the creative side, it does not get made. If a policy does not work for the AI side, we redesign it. The goal is not to referee a fight but to build the infrastructure that makes the fight unnecessary.

What we stand for

Credtent works only if both sides trust it completely.

Every structural, legal, and financial decision traces back to that.

01

Structural neutrality

We don't train models. We don't compete with AI companies. We don't publish content. Our only business is connecting both sides on fair terms.

02

Rights holders first

The 85/15 split reflects our belief that the people who made the content that trains AI deserve most of the value it creates.

03

Ethical by design

Opt-outs are free. Credtent does not plan to charge for the free opt-out. License terms include anti-plagiarism guardrails. Compliance documentation is standard, not optional.

Delaware Public Benefit Corporation

Credtent's charter names two public benefits: to empower and protect creators of original works in the marketplace of artificial intelligence, and to promote the ethical and credible use and deployment of AI. As a Delaware Public Benefit Corporation, its directors must balance those benefits with stockholders' financial interests.

85/15 revenue split

85% of licensing revenue goes to creatives, rights holders, and data providers.

8+ years of research

Research as a service, carried out with a rigorous scientific method.

Who Credtent is

E.R. Burgess and the company he founded.

Credtent connects AI companies and the creative industries through licensing, so AI solutions are ethical and commercially safe.

E.R. Burgess founded Credtent after more than eight years of research into creative rights and data provenance, well before the public AI debate grew loud. The starting idea was simple: AI and human creativity can work together, ethically, legally, and profitably, when the people who make the work are paid and their consent is on record.

E.R. Burgess brings over 30 years of industry experience. He is also a published tabletop game designer, with card, board, and role-playing games to his name. He leads Credtent as Founder and CEO, alongside Co-Founder and Chief Science Officer Dr. Galen Buckwalter.

Our mission is to bring AI and creativity together ethically, legally, and profitably. Credtent, Inc. is a Delaware Public Benefit Corporation and a Certified B Corp. Its charter names protecting creators of original works in the AI marketplace as a public benefit.
