About Credtent

Credtent, Inc. is a Delaware Public Benefit Corporation founded by Eric R. Burgess (CEO) and Dr. Galen Buckwalter.

More choice. Wider participation. We help creatives and AI companies find constructive ways to work together through choice, consent, credit, and compensation.

Human judgment

Use AI with curiosity while keeping creative skills, agency, and responsibility strong.

Neutral role

Help content owners and AI companies understand each other's needs and agree clear terms.

Shared opportunity

Make room for more people to participate in the AI economy and the value their work creates.
