About Credtent

Credtent, Inc. is a Delaware Public Benefit Corporation founded by E.R. Burgess (CEO) and Dr. Galen Buckwalter.

We help creatives and AI companies work together through choice, consent, credit, and compensation.

Human judgment

Use AI with curiosity while keeping creative skills, agency, and responsibility strong.

Neutral role

Help content owners and AI companies understand each other's needs and agree clear terms.

Shared opportunity

Make room for more people to participate in the AI economy and the value their work creates.

The origin

Why was Credtent founded?

Credtent exists because the AI economy is not a zero-sum game. Creatives and AI companies need each other, and they need a neutral place to do business together, ethically, legally, and profitably.

For more than eight years, our team has researched creative IP, digital rights, and the collision between AI's demand for training data and intellectual property law. When AI content licensing reached the mainstream, we had a model ready to test.

What is Credtent's mission?

Credtent's mission is to bring AI and creativity together ethically, legally, and profitably.

We measure our decisions against that mission. We try to make every deal work for the creative side, and when a policy does not work for the AI side, we revise it.

What we stand for

Credtent works only if both sides trust it.

We designed our structure, contracts, and finances to earn that trust.

01

Structural neutrality

We build no AI models, own none of the content we license, and compete with no AI company.

02

Rights holders first

The standard 85/15 split reflects our belief that the people who made the content that trains AI deserve most of the value it creates.

Under Credtent's standard terms, content partners receive 85% of each licensing deal, and the Terms of Service list the costs and taxes deducted before payment. Credtent keeps a 15% platform fee.

03

Ethical by design

License terms bar licensees from reproducing protected work in their outputs, and every license comes with a disclosure pack.

Credtent does not plan to charge for the free opt-out.

Delaware Public Benefit Corporation

Credtent's charter names two public benefits: to empower and protect creators of original works in the marketplace of artificial intelligence, and to promote the ethical and credible use and deployment of AI. The charter also requires Credtent to be managed in a manner that balances stockholders' financial interests, the best interests of those materially affected by its conduct, and those public benefits.

Who Credtent is

E.R. Burgess and the company he founded.

E.R. Burgess founded Credtent and leads it as Founder and CEO, with Co-Founder and Chief Science Officer Dr. Galen Buckwalter.

  • He brings over 30 years of industry experience.
  • He spent more than eight years researching creative rights and data provenance before founding Credtent.
  • He is a published designer of card, board, and role-playing games.

He started from the idea that AI and human creativity can work together, ethically, legally, and profitably, when the people who make the work are paid and their consent is on record.
