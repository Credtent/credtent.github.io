What's covered

What a Credtent license includes.

Each license covers the uses you define and names every work it includes.

  • A named manifest of the licensed works
  • A record of which rights holder warranted which rights
  • A disclosure pack for EU AI Act Art. 53 and California AB 2013 filings
  • One contract that reaches many rights holders

Licenses include the rights the contracting holders warranted, a named manifest, and a disclosure pack your counsel can use for EU AI Act Art. 53 and CA AB 2013 filings. Those statutes still bind the developer. Indemnity, if any, is only as written in the signed license.

Frequently asked questions

What comes with a Credtent license?

The rights the contracting rights holders warranted, a named manifest of the works, and a disclosure pack your counsel can use for EU AI Act Art. 53 and California AB 2013 filings. Those laws still apply to you as the developer, and any indemnity is only what the signed license says.

Can you license content we already trained on?

We can structure a going-forward license and a covenant not to sue over past use from the rights holders who sign, limited to the named works. Neither one binds a rights holder who did not sign, and neither one satisfies the developer's regulatory obligations.

Does citation permission include training?

No. Training and citation in AI answers are agreed separately. A source link on its own does not grant training permission.
