Questions

What does a Credtent license cover?

Licenses include the rights the contracting holders warranted, a named manifest, and a disclosure pack your counsel can use for EU AI Act Art. 53 and CA AB 2013 filings. Those statutes still bind the developer. Indemnity, if any, is only as written in the signed license.

What about content a model already used?

We can structure a going-forward license plus a covenant not to sue from the rights holders who sign, covering the named works. That is not a release from everyone else, and it is not a regulator cure.

Does display permission include training?

No. Training and display permissions are agreed separately. A source link alone does not establish training permission.
