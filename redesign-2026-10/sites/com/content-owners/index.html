What you keep

Your catalog stays yours.

The economics

85% goes to the people whose work was licensed.

Every deal Credtent closes sends 85% of gross licensing revenue to the content owners, rights holders, and data providers who made it possible. The other 15% pays for the platform, legal work, compliance tools, and operations that make the whole system work.

Under the 85/15 split, revenue reaches rights holders every quarter.

Frequently Asked Questions

What do I keep when I list my catalog with Credtent?

You keep full ownership of everything you list. Credtent acts as your licensing agent, not a publisher or co-owner, and holds only limited representation rights for AI licensing that you can end at any time. Listing transfers no copyright and no exclusivity, and every deal needs your approval before it goes ahead.

How does the revenue split work?

Credtent sends 85% of gross revenue from every licensing deal to the rights holders and data providers whose content was licensed, and keeps 15% for the platform, legal work, compliance tools, and operations. The split applies to gross revenue.

When do Credtent partners get paid?

Royalties are paid quarterly. Each payment comes with a usage report showing which licensees used your catalog and how much, so you can tie each payment to specific deals.

Can I get a valuation without a licensing mandate?

Yes. Obtain a valuation independently, then decide whether you want help finding licensing partners.

Where do individual creatives opt out?

Free opt-out for creatives lives on credtent.org and does not require a licensing agreement.
