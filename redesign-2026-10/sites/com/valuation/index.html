Discuss a custom assessment

More than volume or tokens.

Assessment considers intended AI use alongside creative-side evidence. Different uses and rights can change the value discussion.

Creative significance

Cultural significance, subject-matter expertise, distinctive perspective, and other supported indicators.

Audience and recognition

Audience reception, awards, and relevant third-party evidence, with appropriate sources and context.

Scope and intended use

The proposed AI use, rights requested, limitations, and the content available for that agreement.

The context behind a valuation

  1. Intended AI use

    Training, answers, or both

  2. Evidence of success

    Audience reception and awards

  3. Cultural significance

    Context and distinctive contribution

  4. Quality and expertise

    The work and its specialist value

  5. Rights and scope

    Permissions, limits, and exclusivity

A neutral assessment

An informed starting point for what to charge.

Illustrative inputs, not a formula. Evidence, assumptions, and intended use inform the assessment; the parties agree the final price.

An estimate supports a conversation.

The assessment should identify evidence, assumptions, scope, and uncertainty. An estimate is not a guaranteed transaction price or an established industry standard. Final terms depend on the parties and their agreement.

Credtent's proposed approach brings context and supported indicators into constructive pricing discussions. Third-party ratings and evidence must be appropriately sourced; unsupported ratings are not part of this preview.

Valuation can stand on its own.

You may already have a buyer or a licensing agreement. Obtain a valuation independently, then decide whether you want help finding licensing partners or negotiating another opportunity.

Questions

Is a valuation a price?

No. An estimate is not a guaranteed transaction price or an established industry standard. The parties agree the final price.

What is the difference between baseline and custom?

A baseline valuation comes when content is added. Optional paid custom valuation goes deeper into catalog, evidence, and intended use.

Will I see a numeric score?

No. The diagram shows qualitative inputs only. Credtent does not publish weights, scores, or valuation figures.
