    Training, answers, or both

  5. Rights and scope

A neutral assessment

An informed starting point for what to charge.

An estimate supports a conversation.

The assessment should identify evidence, assumptions, scope, and uncertainty. An estimate is not a guaranteed transaction price or an established industry standard. Final terms depend on the parties and their agreement.

Credtent's proposed approach brings context and supported indicators into constructive pricing discussions. Third-party ratings and evidence must be appropriately sourced; unsupported ratings are not part of a Credtent valuation.

Valuation can stand on its own.

You may already have a buyer or a licensing agreement. Obtain a valuation independently, then decide whether you want help finding licensing partners or negotiating another opportunity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a valuation a price?

No. An estimate is not a guaranteed transaction price or an established industry standard. The parties agree the final price.

What is the difference between baseline and custom?

A baseline valuation comes when content is added. Optional paid custom valuation goes deeper into catalog, evidence, and intended use.

Will I see a numeric score?

No. The diagram shows qualitative inputs only. Credtent does not publish weights, scores, or valuation figures.
