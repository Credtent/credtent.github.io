Content valuation

Understand your content's value. Explore your licensing options.

Credtent assesses what content is worth for AI licensing, so that high-quality, credible work is licensed at an appropriate value.

A baseline valuation comes when you register on Credtent. Optional paid custom valuation goes deeper, into the catalog, its evidence, and its intended use.

The Burgess-Buckwalter methodology, the Credtent Universal Content Valuation System

  1. Intended AI use

    Training, answers, or both

  2. Evidence of success

    Audience reception and awards

  3. Cultural significance

    Context and distinctive contribution

  4. Quality and expertise

    The work and its specialist value

  5. Rights and scope

    Permissions, limits, and exclusivity

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Credtent?

Credtent is a neutral marketplace that helps content owners and AI companies agree permitted AI uses, value content, and license it for training and for use in AI answers.

Does a license replace a company's own legal duties?

Licenses include the rights the contracting holders warranted, a named manifest, and a disclosure pack your counsel can use for EU AI Act Art. 53 and CA AB 2013 filings. Those statutes still bind the developer. Indemnity, if any, is only as written in the signed license.

Are source visits guaranteed?

Source visits are a goal, not a guarantee. Training and display permissions are agreed separately.
