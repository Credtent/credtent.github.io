## Astra redesign PDF review (added 2026-10-25)
Source: "Credtent design review 07" (21 pages: 8 page types, desktop + mobile, static mockups dated Sep 21, 2026), plus credtent-website-analysis.md, revision06-brief.md and revision07-feedback-checklist.md.

**Direction and audiences.** Two sites. .org is for individual creatives: control and understand AI use of their work. Its main action is a free opt-out; it adds Opt-Out Premium (monthly audits, US$49 a year), Creative Consent platform ratings, resources and platform-policy update articles. The tone is calm and humane. .com is for content owners and AI companies: licensing, rights, valuation and payments. Its actions are Sign up and Contact us; the tone is concrete and commercial. The two sites link to each other. Opting out is presented as a complete choice, with no pressure to license.

**Messaging.** .org: "Your work. Your choices. Your terms." .com: "A neutral marketplace connecting content owners and AI companies." Other lines: "Your work. New ways to earn." (four licensing steps), "Agree how AI can use your content." (full-cycle licensing), and for GRR citation, "More control over your content in AI answers." Unsupported certification, indemnification and compliance claims are dropped.

**Page structure.** .org home; .com home; Creative Consent; citation licensing (GRR); content valuation (a five-input qualitative diagram with no prices or weights); AI training licensing; About; Collaborithmic.com.

**Visual design.** Brand purple #290099; orange #EC5A00 buttons. Nunito Sans on .com; Georgia/Arial on .org (a font conflict that is still unresolved). Real licensed Pexels/Unsplash photos, each used in one slot only; no generated images; official logos re-exported. Semantic HTML with honest structured data; no special "AI SEO" tricks.

**Revisions.** r04 adjusted spacing and hierarchy. r05 swapped in real photos, set the neutral-marketplace headline, added the valuation graphic, and reworked the citation and AI-company copy. r06 brought a professional bridge-handshake hero, a manuscript photo for the content owner, and merged the citation and AI sections into one "Full-cycle licensing" section. r07 changed only the .com hero (a photographer-handshake photo, 16:9).

**Reusable now.** The .com/.org audience split already matches Eric's plan (corporate .com, B2C/resources .org). Reusable as-is: the comparison table, the .com valuation, training and citation pages, the .org opt-out, Premium, Creative Consent and resources layout, the claims discipline, and the SEO handoff. Still open before launch: the team roster (lists Deborah, now stale), a $14.99/$24.99/$49 price check, the valuation method, and the platform guide.
