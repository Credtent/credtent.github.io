What this badge is, and is not. The badge builder produces a free disclosure badge: you declare your own Creative Origin classification, and the badge carries that self-declared disclosure in readable metadata. It is not a Credtent certification. Certified badges come through Credtent's paid, expert-reviewed certification process, which verifies your classification and adds a registry record.
