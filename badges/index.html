Badges

The badge systems.

Three badge systems, one for each side of the rights economy. All certification is human-led and expert-led, and applies to works and organizational processes, not individuals.

For Creators

Creative Origin

Signals the role of AI in a specific creative work.

  • HCC (Human Composed Creation): the work was composed by a human.
  • AAC (AI Assisted Creation): a human composed the work with AI assistance.
  • ACC (AI Composed Creation): the work was composed by AI.
For Platforms

Creative Consent

Rates how a platform uses your data, content, and metadata, and whether it respects your work with regard to AI training.

  • Tiers: CCF, CCO, CCN.
For AI Companies

Commercial Safety

IP-safety certification for AI models.

  • Levels: Full, Guardrail, Provisional.

Our badges are free to use. We charge for expert-led certification because expert review is real labor and deserves real compensation. Looking for the wider landscape? Browse the AI disclosure standards catalog.
