The badge systems

Three badge systems, one for each side of the rights economy. All certification is human-led and expert-led, and applies to works and organizational processes, not individuals.

For Creators and Publishers

Creative Origin

Signals the role of AI in a specific creative work. For creators and publishers who want their audience to know how a work was made, from a fully human-composed novel to an AI-generated image, with the middle ground disclosed honestly.

  • Human Composed: the work was composed by a human, without generative AI composing it.
  • AI Assisted: a person made the work with help from AI tools.
  • AI Generated: the work was generated by AI.
  • Legacy Creation: the work was made before November 2022, predating generative AI tools.

Two ways to display it: a free self-declared disclosure badge you generate yourself, or a paid, expert-reviewed certified badge with a registry record.

For Platforms

Creative Consent

Demonstrates that a platform licenses and uses creator content on rights-compliant terms: how it treats creator data, content, and metadata, and whether it respects creators' choices about AI training. For platforms that want to show creators, in a standardized way, that consent is honored.

  • Full Protection: the platform does not use creator content for AI training, does not use it internally for generative AI, and does not share it with third parties for AI training.
  • Optional Protection: the platform gives users a choice about AI training use, with a clear, cost-free, and simple opt-out.
  • No Protection: the platform uses creator content for AI training without consent or compensation and offers no opt-out.

Badge questions

What are Credtent's three badge systems?

Creative Origin, for creators and publishers, discloses how AI was involved in a work. Creative Consent, for platforms, rates how a platform treats creator content and AI training choices at Full, Optional, or No Protection. Commercial Safety, for AI companies, certifies training practices at Full, Guardrail, or Provisional level.

Are Credtent badges free?

Credtent's disclosure badges are free to use, and a self-declared Creative Origin badge can be made with the free badge builder in about a minute without an account. Expert-led certification is paid, because expert review is real labor.

Does Credtent certify people?

Credtent certifies works and organizational processes. Certification is human-led and expert-led.

Is Credtent part of the Content Credentials ecosystem?

Credtent is a member of the Content Authenticity Initiative (CAI), the community that promotes adoption of Content Credentials. Credtent's badges work alongside Content Credentials by recording the result of an expert review of authorship or licensing.
