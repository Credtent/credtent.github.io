Credtent.org

Resources for the rights economy.

Credtent is a Public Benefit Corporation building valuation, certification, and licensing infrastructure for human-created content. This site is the resource side of that work: free opt-out filing, the badge systems, and the standards catalog. The commercial side lives at credtent.com.

Free, Always

Opt out of AI training.

You do not have to license anything to anyone. If you want your work excluded from AI training, file an opt-out request here. It is free, it takes minutes, and we will tell you exactly what happens after you file.

Badges

The badge systems.

Three badge systems, one for each side of the rights economy. All certification is human-led and expert-led, and applies to works and organizational processes, not individuals.

Creative Origin

For creators: signals the role of AI in a specific creative work.

Tiers: HCC, AAC, ACC

Creative Consent

For platforms: rates how a platform uses your data, content, and metadata, and whether it respects your work with regard to AI training.

Tiers: CCF, CCO, CCN

Commercial Safety

For AI companies: IP-safety certification for AI models.

Levels: Full, Guardrail, Provisional
