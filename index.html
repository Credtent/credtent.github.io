What is Credtent?

Credtent is the neutral registry and rights clearinghouse that sits between the people who make content and the companies that want to use it. Rights holders register a work, prove where it came from, and set the terms on which AI can use it. AI companies license content with the chain of title already cleared and the rights holder already paid. Credtent builds no AI models and competes with neither side, which is why both sides can trust the same rails.

Where the Rights Still Have Teeth

The value that training law does not reach

Training law is moving. A court may decide that training on lawfully acquired text is fair use, and if it does, the license to train on plain text gets cheaper. That is not where the durable value sits, and we are not going to pretend otherwise.

The rights that hold are the ones with their own laws behind them: a person's voice, their likeness, their performance, and the right to reproduce, display, or cite a work rather than just learn from it. An AI buyer needs those cleared no matter which way the training cases break.

Creative Origin

Where did this come from. A neutral, third-party record of a work's provenance.

Creative Consent

Did the creator agree to this use. Consent recorded at the source, per work.

Commercial Safety

Is it cleared to use commercially. The answer a buyer or a court eventually asks for.
