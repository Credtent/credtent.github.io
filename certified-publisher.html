What the Badge Means

Verified Process, Not Just a Score

Creative Origin is not an AI detector. Detectors score finished text for statistical signatures, and can be fooled. Credtent reviews the editorial process behind the content: who wrote it, who edited it, what tools were used, and how oversight was maintained at every stage.

Human Authorship Verified

Credtent reviewers confirm the content was conceived and written by human authors. Author attestations are on file in the certification record.

Editorial Process Documented

We review the full editorial workflow: assignment, drafting, editing, fact-checking, and publication. Context is what makes this certification defensible.

AI Use Disclosed

If AI tools were used in any part of the process, they are identified and disclosed in the certification record. Transparency is required; absence of AI is not.

Independent Reviewer

Reviews are conducted by Credtent-trained practitioners from editorial, journalism, and media backgrounds. You get a named reviewer, not a queue.

Queryable Public Record

Every badge links to a public certification record in the Credtent registry. Readers, buyers, and platforms can verify it independently at any time.

Annual Renewal

Standards evolve as the industry does. Annual renewal keeps the certification current and the badge valid across distribution partners and platforms.

How It Works

Expert Review, Not Automated Scoring

Certification is human-led by practitioners with backgrounds in editorial, journalism, and publishing. The process takes your organizational context seriously, at the same quality bar AI can never apply.

Step 01

Submit Your Process

Provide editorial process documentation, author or editorial attestations, and your AI use policy. Our reviewer team contacts you within five business days.

Step 02

Expert Review

A Credtent-trained practitioner evaluates your documentation against the Creative Origin standard. Questions go to a named reviewer, not a ticket queue.

Step 03

Certification Decision

You receive a detailed review letter. Certified organizations get a badge, a public certification record in the Credtent registry, and display rights.
