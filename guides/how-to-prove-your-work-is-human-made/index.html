How to Prove Your Work Is Human Made (2026 Guide)

Updated September 2026. Published by Credtent, a Delaware Public Benefit Corporation.

Credtent / Guides / How to Prove Your Work Is Human Made

To prove your work is human made, keep the evidence of how you made it (dated drafts, version history, research notes, and editor correspondence), disclose any AI tool you used, and have an independent expert review that record. Credtent's Creative Origin certification performs that review and issues a certified Human Composed badge linked to a public registry record. A detector score cannot settle authorship on its own, because detectors measure how predictable prose is.

Readers, buyers, and platforms now ask a question that used to go without saying: did a person make this? The answer that holds up is a documented process with a qualified reviewer behind it. This guide covers what counts as proof, where AI detectors fit, and how Credtent certifies human authorship.

What counts as proof that a human made a work?

Proof of human authorship is a record of the making: dated drafts, version history, outlines, research notes, and correspondence with editors, plus a signed statement from the author about any AI tools used. A record of process is much harder to fabricate than a finished file. For works created before November 2022, the creation date is itself strong evidence, and Credtent marks those works with its Legacy Creation tier, which records that they were made before generative AI tools became widely available.

The most useful evidence is the evidence you already produce while working. Save drafts under dated file names, or work in a tool that keeps version history, and keep your notes, research links, and the email thread with your editor.

If you use software that touches the text, write down what it did. Under Credtent's standard, routine tool assistance where you still make the final composition decisions counts as incidental AI use, which carries no disclosure obligation and does not change the tier. If a tool generated or rewrote your prose, disclose it.

Can an AI detector prove that writing is human?

An AI detector cannot prove human authorship by itself. Detectors estimate how statistically predictable a passage is, and careful human writing, such as research or technical prose, can score as machine-like. In Credtent's certification, a high detector reading triggers a request for process evidence, an expert reviewer makes the decision, and the detector score stays in the audit trail. The certificate shows the tier and the basis for it, and it never shows a detector score.

Credtent treats detectors as a triage tool, using several analyses together, and a trained reviewer who understands the field reads the evidence in context. A 70 to 80 percent detector reading on human-authored research does not move the tier when the drafts and notes show a person did the work.

What is Credtent's Creative Origin badge?

Creative Origin is Credtent's badge system for disclosing the role of AI in a creative work. It has four tiers: Human Composed, AI-Assisted, AI-Generated, and Legacy Creation for works made before November 2022. Anyone can generate a free self-declared badge. Human Composed and AI-Assisted can also be certified after an expert review, and each certified badge links to a public record in the Credtent registry.

Each badge names the part of the work it describes (text, art, music, code, video, or voices). A novel can carry a Human Composed badge for its text and an AI-Assisted badge for its cover, so readers see exactly where AI was involved.

You can build a free self-declared badge with the Credtent badge builder. The full system is on the badge systems page.

How does Creative Origin certification work?

Creative Origin certification reviews the process behind a work or behind an organization's editorial workflow. You submit drafts or process documentation, author attestations, and your AI use policy. A Credtent-trained practitioner with an editorial, journalism, or publishing background reviews the record against the Creative Origin standard and issues a decision letter. Certified organizations receive a badge, a public registry record, and display rights, and they renew annually.

  1. Submit your process. Provide editorial documentation, attestations, and your AI use policy. Credtent's reviewer team contacts you within five business days.
  2. Expert review. A named reviewer evaluates the documentation. Questions go to that person.
  3. Decision. You receive a detailed review letter. Certified work and organizations get a badge and a queryable record in the Credtent registry.
  4. Annual renewal. Tools change quickly, so organizational certifications renew each year.

Credtent's certification process was reviewed and accepted by the Writers Guild of America, and WGA members receive a discount on certification fees. Start at Creative Origin certification.

How does human-made certification relate to AI disclosure rules?

The EU AI Act's Article 50 sets transparency duties for certain AI-generated content, which separates AI-generated material from everything else. Credtent's Creative Origin tiers map onto that distinction and add more detail: they show whether AI was absent, assisted, or generated the work, and which part of the work each answer covers. A certified badge gives a publisher an independent record of that disclosure.

Credtent's tiers are Credtent's own system, which maps onto Article 50; the regulation itself does not require three or four tiers.

Frequently asked questions

Does using spell-check or a grammar tool mean my work is not human made?

Routine tool assistance, where you still make the final composition decisions, counts as incidental AI use under Credtent's standard and does not change a Creative Origin tier. If a tool generates or rewrites your prose, disclose it, and the work may belong in the AI-Assisted tier.

Does the Credtent certificate show an AI-detection score?

The certificate shows the Creative Origin tier and the basis for it. Detector readings stay in Credtent's audit trail, and an expert reviewer, supported by process evidence, makes the certification decision.

Can I prove a work is human made if I wrote it before generative AI tools were widely available?

Works created before November 2022 qualify for Credtent's Legacy Creation tier, which records that the work was made before generative AI tools became widely available. Legacy Creation is a self-declared badge. The date of creation, supported by your records, is the evidence.

Does Credtent certify people as human creators?

Credtent certifies works and organizational processes. The badge attaches to a specific work, or to an organization's editorial process, so the claim stays tied to something a reviewer examined.

Can books and articles get human authorship certification?

Credtent certifies the editorial processes of publishers, newsrooms, trade media, and newsletters today. Certification for individual books is in development.

How much does it cost to prove my work is human made?

A self-declared Creative Origin badge is free. Expert certification is paid, because expert review is real labor, and the fee depends on scope. Credtent returns a certification scope and fee estimate within five business days of an inquiry.
