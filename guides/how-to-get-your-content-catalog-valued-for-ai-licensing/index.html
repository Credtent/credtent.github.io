How to Get Your Content Catalog Valued for AI Licensing (2026 Guide)

Updated September 2026. Published by Credtent, a Delaware Public Benefit Corporation.

Credtent / Guides / How to Get Your Content Catalog Valued for AI Licensing

To get your content catalog valued for AI licensing, start with a free automated baseline estimate, then commission a paid expert-led valuation before you negotiate. Free broker estimates price what AI buyers pay for: format, volume, and training utility. Credtent's valuation also weighs creative merit, human authorship, and real-world validation such as awards, sales, and critical recognition, because Credtent's view is that quality work is what produces better models.

A catalog owner's first number now arrives quickly and for free. That number is useful, and it is incomplete. This guide explains what automated estimates measure, what an expert-led valuation adds, and how to get one from Credtent.

What do free AI data valuations measure?

Free instant valuations from AI data brokers measure what AI buyers pay for: file format, volume, and how much training work a dataset can support. Those inputs are real, but they leave creative merit out of the model, so an Academy Award-winning film can land in the same band as a video of cats playing in the yard, and an album that won Grammys and sold 10 million copies can price like a recording of a storm at night.

A broker's estimate is built for the buyer's side of the table, and brokers are paid when a deal closes. The number can still be accurate on its own terms, which is why it is worth understanding what it leaves out before you sign anything.

What is an automated baseline valuation?

An automated baseline valuation is a quick estimate of a catalog's annual AI licensing value, calculated from the details you provide about the work. Credtent Aegis produces one free when you register your works: a low, middle, and high range with a confidence rating and a short note on how the range was reached. It also records any awards, ratings, sales figures, and audience reach you mention, so that evidence carries forward.

A baseline is a fair starting point, and Credtent offers it free for the same reason brokers do: owners deserve a number early. Used alone, an automated number treats a creator's work as a commodity. That is why Credtent treats the free baseline as the first of two steps, and the paid expert-led valuation as the second.

What does an expert-led valuation add?

An expert-led valuation adds the judgment an automated estimate cannot supply. Credtent's named content valuation experts, founder Eric Burgess and co-founder Dr. Galen Buckwalter, assess each collection for creative merit, human authorship, cultural and professional footprint, market demand, exclusivity, and real-world validation such as awards, sales, and recognition. Credtent's aim is to be the best at quantifying what is hard to quantify.

Eric Burgess and Dr. Galen Buckwalter have spent more than a decade building ways to measure things that resist a single number. Together they built the Earned Media Value Index, a psychometric method for putting a cash value on social media actions and endorsements, which continues today as the Social Index. They also co-created the Brand Trust Index, and they maintain Cultural Value and Personal Value Indexes. Those rubrics feed Credtent's valuation method, which scores a catalog on content quality and authority, market demand, exclusivity and substitutability, cultural and professional footprint, and success metrics, then anchors the scores to verified market behavior.

Dr. Galen Buckwalter is Credtent's co-founder and Chief Science Officer (CSCO). He was eHarmony's founding Chief Science Officer and lead inventor on the patent behind its matching algorithm. Every expert valuation is a written report with value ranges by collection and the method and assumptions stated, so a rights holder, a board, or a buyer's team can follow the reasoning and challenge it.

Why does creative quality matter to AI licensing value?

Credtent's position is that human-made, well-regarded work trains better models. Awards, sales, critical reception, and lasting audiences are evidence from the world that a work is high quality, and AI developers need high-quality data as the supply of open web content runs thin. Credtent's valuation counts that evidence, which gives creators and rights holders a number that reflects what matters to them as well as to the buyer.

Human authorship is disclosed independently through Credtent's Creative Origin badge, the same evidence a valuation weighs.

A valuation that counts only bytes and hours can price a masterpiece and a test clip within the same range. Counting the human elements (who made the work, how it was received, what it has earned) is how a valuation reflects the work's actual standing.

How do I get my catalog valued by Credtent?

Register your works in Credtent Aegis and review the free automated baseline. Then request a paid expert-led valuation and describe the catalog: content types, number of works, where the collection lives, and who holds the rights. Credtent's fees scale with catalog size. Credtent collects information about the collection, never the files themselves, and a person performs every expert valuation.

  1. Register and review the free baseline. Your registration records the catalog details and any awards, sales, and audience figures you share.
  2. Request the paid expert valuation. Describe the collection and its rights. Individual creators and small publishers start at the entry tiers; larger publishers and media companies move to a scoped engagement.
  3. Receive the report. Value ranges by collection, the method, and the assumptions, written for use in a negotiation.
  4. Decide what to do next. A valuation stands on its own. If you later list the catalog with Credtent, you keep 85 percent of every licensing deal Credtent closes.
