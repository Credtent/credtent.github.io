Plans & Pricing

Pricing That Scales With You

Custom licensing for AI companies, straightforward revenue terms for content partners, and a free opt-out path for individual creators.

How much does Credtent cost?

Credtent pricing depends on which side of the market you are on. AI companies pay custom enterprise licensing priced per deal. Content partners and rights holders keep 85% of every licensing deal Credtent closes, with Credtent retaining 15%, and a platform fee applies. Individual creators can opt out for free, add auditing with Opt-Out Premium at $49 per year, or license their work with Creator Licensing at $99 per year.

Enterprise Licensing for AI

How much does Credtent cost for AI companies?

AI companies license training data from Credtent through custom enterprise agreements; there is no fixed public price. Pricing is scoped to the content, the license type, and the size of the organization, because every corpus and use case differs. Each agreement includes a disclosure pack, and pricing is set during a scoping conversation rather than from a published rate card.

Enterprise licensing fees are scoped to the deal, based on content volume, license type, and organization size. There's no public price list because no two deals are the same. What we can promise: neutral terms, fast deal cycles, and compliance documentation included.

Content Partners

How much does Credtent cost for content partners?

A platform fee applies for content partners, and Credtent retains 15% of each licensing deal it closes and routes the other 85% to the rights holder, under the Starter Partner terms. The Organization License keeps the same 85/15 split and adds enhanced services for larger catalogs, and Enterprise is custom-priced for the largest catalogs. Partners earn when Credtent closes a deal.

The tier you choose determines the level of service and support you receive.

Individual Creators

How much does Credtent cost for individual creators?

Individual creators can use Credtent's opt-out for free, forever, with no cost to register works for the industry-wide opt-out. Opt-Out Premium at $49 per year adds access to the auditing system but does not include licensing. Licensing is a separate set of plans: Creator Licensing at $99 per year, and Rights-Holder Licensing starting at $299 per year.

Every creator gets a free path to opt out. Add auditing to see where your opt-out stands, or choose a licensing plan to start earning from AI licensing.
