The Economics

How does Credtent's revenue split work for rights holders?

Credtent routes 85% of gross revenue from every licensing deal directly to the rights holders and data providers whose content was licensed, and retains 15% for platform, legal infrastructure, compliance tooling, and operations. The split is written into every contract and applied to gross revenue.

Every deal Credtent closes returns 85% of licensing revenue to the content owners, rights holders, and data providers who made it possible. The other 15% funds the infrastructure that makes the whole system work.

Built Into Every Contract

The 85/15 split isn't a negotiating position. It's structural. Every licensing agreement Credtent executes, regardless of deal size, routes revenue to rights holders on a quarterly cadence. No exceptions.

How It Works

How do I list my catalog with Credtent?

Listing a catalog with Credtent is a four-step process: submit your catalog, Credtent provenance-certifies the content, Credtent closes licensing deals on your behalf, and royalties are distributed to you quarterly under the 85/15 split. You register once and earn on every deal Credtent closes against your catalog, with no platform fee to list.
