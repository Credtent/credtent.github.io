Retroactive Licensing

Already trained on content you did not license? Credtent clears it.

Retroactive licensing brings past use into good standing and covers it going forward, so a sourcing gap you inherited does not stay an open liability. Credtent identifies the rights holders, negotiates a license covering the prior use, and provides the documentation that puts the relationship on legitimate footing.

This is a live part of the standard offer, with indemnification for past unauthorized use written into the first-year license. It converts an undocumented position into a licensed, auditable one, structured with the same rigor as a prospective agreement.
